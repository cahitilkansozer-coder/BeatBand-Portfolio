01 / The experience

Two game modes.
One performance.

BeatBand connects timing-based dance gameplay with puzzle strategy, creating a single audition-style journey.

01 — DANCE

Hit the beat

Respond to LEFT, RIGHT, and UP notes synchronized to music. Earn PERFECT, GREAT, or GOOD judgments and build your combo.

02 — VOCAL

Solve the puzzle

Swap music-themed tiles, create power-ups, clear obstacles, and navigate cascades while building Vocal Energy.

03 — PERFORMANCE

Own the stage

Transition through Signature Move, Vocal Performance, High Note, and Results with Sophie and the stage.

02 / What I built

Designed, built, tested.

The prototype combines gameplay programming with custom authoring and testing workflows.

Rhythm & Stage Flow

Music-timed notes, judgments, character transitions and performance phases.

Match-3 Systems

Power-ups, board masks, obstacles, gravity, cascades and energy.

Custom Level Editor

Author and validate rhythm charts, puzzle layouts and isolated tests.

03 / Engineering & iteration

Product thinking meets technical execution.

A modular Unity/C# prototype built through iterative implementation, gameplay observation and regression tests. Technical work includes coordinating multiple gameplay phases, deterministic board rules, custom level design tools and safer versioned integrations.

Current status: playable development prototype.
